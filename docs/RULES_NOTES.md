# Hackathon Rules Notes

Source: https://paypalaihackathon.devpost.com/rules (read on Nov 3, 2026)

## Dates
- Submission closes: Nov 12, 2026, 12:00 pm Pacific = 11:00 pm EAT (Nairobi)
- My internal target: submit by Nov 11
- Judging: Dec 1 to Dec 15, 2026
- Winners announced: around Dec 21, 2026

## Eligibility
- Age of majority where I live. Kenya is not on the excluded list
  (excluded: Brazil, Quebec, Russia, Crimea, Cuba, Iran, North Korea,
  and OFAC-sanctioned countries).
- Individuals, teams and organizations can enter.

## Project requirements
- Must meaningfully use PayPal (free sandbox) AND an AI tool or model.
- Must be a working project, not a mockup or static prototype.
- Existing code must be significantly updated during the hackathon, and I
  must explain how. This repo is new (started Nov 3, 2026).

## Submission checklist
- [ ] Text description of features and functionality
- [ ] Public GitHub repo with an open source license visible in About (MIT)
- [ ] Working demo: hosted URL OR complete setup/run instructions in the repo
- [ ] Test credentials / sandbox logins in the README
- [ ] Demo video: under 3 minutes, public on YouTube, link on the form
- [ ] Video has no copyrighted music or third-party trademarks
- [ ] Tools list: which AI models/services were used and how
- [ ] Everything in English

## Prizes
- One project can win: 1 Grand Prize + 1 Sponsor Prize,
  OR 1 Honorable Mention + 1 Sponsor Prize.
- Judging: stage one is pass/fail on theme fit; stage two scores five equally
  weighted criteria: Technological Implementation, Design, Potential Impact,
  Innovation/Idea, Presentation.

## After submitting
- Keep the demo, sandbox accounts and AI credit working until Dec 15.
- Winners may need a W-8BEN form and pay their own wire fees and local taxes.
